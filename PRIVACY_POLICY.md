## Summary

Heard does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All processing happens on your device.

---

## Data Collection

Heard collects **no personal data** of any kind. There are no user accounts, no registration, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Microphone Access

The app requires microphone permission solely to capture speech and convert it to captions in real time. Audio is processed entirely on-device using Android's built-in Speech Recognition API. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

## Local Network

Caption text is broadcast over your local Wi-Fi network (port 8765) so you can view captions in a browser on the same network. This is a local-only connection — no data leaves your local network or is sent to the internet.

## Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `RECORD_AUDIO` | Capture speech for real-time captioning |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Show a persistent status notification while captions are running (optional — captions work without it) |
| `INTERNET` | Serve the local caption web page on your Wi-Fi network |
| `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` | Detect your device's local IP address to display the caption URL |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Keep the caption service running while the app is in the background |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Optionally keep the screen on during a caption session |

## Data Retention

No data is retained. The app holds caption text in memory only for as long as the current session is active. When the session ends, all caption text is discarded.

## Children's Privacy

Heard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
